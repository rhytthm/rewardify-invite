The gap

253M Indian kids.
Nobody teaches them money.

Schools don't. The CBSE elective code 904 exists on paper but reaches a tiny slice of 28,500 affiliated schools. Parents feel underqualified — only 27% of Indian adults are themselves financially literate. And the kids fintech wave that raised $50M+ in 2019–22 was a card race, not a literacy race. Five of them are now dead.

◆ The solution

A garden where every plant
is a real financial product.

Kids earn coins by reading, finishing homework, hitting fitness goals, helping siblings, or practising an instrument — the activities Indian parents already pay for. They plant those coins. Each seed tracks the rate of a real product. Weekly weather events teach risk and diversification the way no textbook can: by making the kid feel it.
